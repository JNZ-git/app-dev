## The Yakuza series

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Yakuza, also known as **Like a Dragon** is a favorite video game series of mine. Here, they incorporates the elements of action-adventure, beat-em-up, and the *role-playing* genre.

This game follows a **crime drama** plot inspired by the Japanese Yakuzas. The series is mainly led by the main protagonist named **_Kiryu Kazuma_**, a reformed yakuza associated with the Kanto-based Yakuza called **Tojo Clan**. Despite our main protagonist often finds himself facing the dilemmas of being a Yakuza, the primary theme of the series is his desire to leave the Yakuza for good and start over to assimilate into civilian life. 

The gameplay of Yakuza has the player controlling the protagonist in an open world where the **player** ~~has to~~ can fight random punks and gangsters loitering around **Kamurocho** to earn *experience* and *money* to learn new moves in order to easily progress through the main story. Aside from doing the main story and roaming around beating punks, players can also do side missions to discover humorous interactions with random characters and side contents for additional entertainment like:

The series is well known for the contrast between the dramatic story line and the humorous side content. The franchise has become a commercial and critical success, and as of 2024, Sega has reported that the video game series has sold a combined total of 27.7 million units in physical and digital sales since its debut in 2005[^note]
